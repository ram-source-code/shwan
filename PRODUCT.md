## Platform

web

## Stack

PHP 8.4 (Hostinger shared hosting, LiteSpeed, `.htaccess`), server-rendered HTML, plain CSS, minimal vanilla JS. No build step, no CDN, no database (confirmed: links-only contact). Follows WEBSITE-BUILD-SPEC.md.

## Users

Businesses and organisations in Sulaymaniyah and the Kurdistan Region looking for a local software and IT partner. They arrive mostly on phones (Facebook links, word of mouth), want to see who the company is and how to reach them, then call, WhatsApp, or email.

## Product Purpose

Company website for Entersoftgroup, a software and IT company in Sulaymaniyah (Suli). It replaces an unfinished theme-based site. Success: a visitor understands what Entersoftgroup does, trusts that it is a real local team, and contacts them.

## Positioning

A Suli-based team (founded by Shwan Baglama) with 15+ years of experience and 99+ projects, whose software ships in Kurdish, Arabic and English, is customised to the client's corporate design, and is supported 24/7 locally.

## Product Principles

1. Real over impressive: only facts the company has stated.
2. Contact is one tap away on every page (call, WhatsApp, email).
3. Local first: a Suli company, findable and reachable.
4. Fast on mid-range phones and weak mobile networks.
