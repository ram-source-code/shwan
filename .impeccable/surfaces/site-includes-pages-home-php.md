# Surface: Entersoftgroup company site (home, about, team, contact) in ckb (default, RTL), en, ar (RTL)

Scope: whole public site, mode Persuade. Audience: Suli businesses choosing a local software/IT partner, mostly on phones, reading Kurdish first. Action: call, WhatsApp or email. Proof: real team of four, real training-room photo, stated facts (15+ years, 99+ projects, software in Kurdish/Arabic/English, 24/7 support). Constraints: no DB, no form, PHP 8.4 Hostinger, no stock photos, no fabricated proof, NO one-sided borders or stripes (user rule), Light/Dark/Auto theme (default Auto).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a two-colour proof sheet from a Sulaymaniyah print shop that sets every line in three scripts; it refuses the IT-category default (blue gradient, laptop hero, icon-card grid) and the vintage-newspaper costume.
OWN-WORLD: Bright white stock (light) / navy-black printing plate (dark); only two inks, the logo's navy #0c3f68 and orange #f7941e, plus their overprint. Heavy wood-type Latin display (Archivo 800+) and a strong Arabic-script display face; boxed frames (four sides), registration marks, printer's ornaments drawn from the logo cube-and-S. No hairline column rules (translated: separation by full frames, ornament rows and space).
STORY: The visitor reads who Entersoftgroup is in their own script, sees the same claim set in all three scripts (the product's truth), meets the real team, and calls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead: logo + site name in Kurdish/Arabic/English. Below it, the headline set huge in the page language, with the other two scripts set small as a proof strip; Call + WhatsApp as stamped ink buttons; facts 15+/99+ in labelled boxes. Founder portrait printed as a duotone (navy ink + orange) inside a framed plate.
FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (Suli trilingual press); seed:62f07d29bd8e264a
Raises: one darkest focal element per viewport (from fluid-ink basin, declined); every fact carries its data label (from brine-jar labels, declined); one strict grid for all content (from teletext, declined); each section front states one claim, detail on its page (from toy window box, declined). Competitive alternate: trilingual silkscreen (multilingual border), not chosen.
Signature interaction: language switch re-inks the masthead (press-impression motion, view transition); theme switch swaps stock for plate. Motion off under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
